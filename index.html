THE PROBLEM

Important information can be scattered.

Students may receive notices through several channels such as class groups, department messages, notice boards and direct communication. When information is spread out, a student can miss a deadline, room change or other important update.

People affected: students, lecturers, class representatives and departments.

What needs to improve?

  • Students need one clear place to check important notices.
  • Updates should be easy to find and understand.
  • Students should be able to see deadlines and reminders.
  • Departments should have a simple way to publish approved notices.

OUR PROPOSED SYSTEM

CampusConnect

A proposed central communication platform for students and staff. It would organize important school announcements, academic deadlines, timetable updates and reminders in one place.

01

Central notices

Approved school updates can be published in one organized location.

02

Deadline reminders

Students can quickly see upcoming assignments, tests and other deadlines.

03

Search & categories

Notices can be grouped by department, programme or type.

04

Clear updates

Important changes such as rooms or schedules can be highlighted.
